What You'll Build

A tool built by a CA, for CAs. Five phases — each one useful on its own.

This isn't a monolithic project that has to be finished before it's useful. Each phase delivers real value the moment it's done.

Phase 1

Upload Bank PDFs, Get Categorized Excel Summaries

Drop in PDF statements from ICICI, SBI, Kotak, IDBI, India Post — or any bank. The tool reads them, categorizes every transaction (salary, rent, EMI, professional fees), and generates a clean, CA-ready Excel summary organized by financial year. Monthly buckets, running balances, native Excel formulas — not hardcoded numbers.

Saves 2-4 hours per statement
Phase 2

Upload 26AS + AIS, Get Automatic Reconciliation

The tool reads your client's Form 26AS and AIS, then cross-references every TDS entry, interest credit, and dividend against the bank statements. Color-coded matches: green for exact, yellow for approximate, red for unmatched. It flags discrepancies before they become notices — "AIS shows FD interest of ₹45,000 from HDFC, but no matching credit found. Is there an HDFC account not uploaded?"

Saves 2-3 hours per client
Phase 3

Smart "What's Missing?" Checklist

Upload everything you have — bank statements, 26AS, AIS, Form 16, investment proofs. The tool looks at what's there and tells you what's not: "Bank shows salary credits but no Form 16 uploaded." "AIS shows MF redemptions — CAMS statement needed." "Bank has LIC debit of ₹45,000 — premium receipt needed for 80C." It even recommends the correct ITR form.

No more chasing documents you forgot to ask for
Phase 4

Auto-Computed Tax, Regime Comparison, Follow-up Questions

All income aggregated under the right heads. Deductions mapped to correct sections. Tax computed under both old and new regime with a side-by-side comparison. And the tool generates smart follow-up questions for your client: "₹3,20,000 received from XYZ Corp — is this salary, professional fees, or personal?" "Cash deposits exceed ₹10L — source documentation needed."

From raw documents to ITR-ready analysis
Phase 5

Multi-User Office Tool

Login system, role-based access, workflow tracking. Assign clients to employees, track who's at which stage, carry forward last year's category mappings. Your whole team uses the same tool. Export ITR-ready data, reconciliation reports, and client communication packages.

Your practice, systematized
Key insight

Each phase is independently useful. Phase 1 alone saves hours this tax season. Build at your own pace — each phase makes the next one more powerful.

This gets built without hiring a developer.

No off-the-shelf software that doesn't understand your workflow. An AI writes the code; you provide the domain expertise.
