The Problem You Already Know

Tax season is a grind. You've lived this every year. Let's name it out loud.

Every assessment year, the same story plays out in CA offices across India. Clients trickle in with bank statements from four different banks, a Form 16 that arrived late, and a vague memory of "some mutual fund redemption last July." Your job is to turn this chaos into a precise, reconciled, error-free tax return. And you have about three months to do it for every single client.

60-70 Hours per week during peak season
2-4 hrs Per bank statement, manually
4-7 Different tools per client filing
1.5L+ AIS/ITR mismatch notices in 2024

The Reconciliation Nightmare

The real pain isn't any single step — it's the constant cross-referencing. You're jumping between the client's bank statements, their AIS download, Form 26AS from TRACES, Form 16 from their employer, and whatever investment proofs they managed to find.

All of this lives in Excel. Multiple tabs, manual lookups, VLOOKUP formulas that break when the format changes. You're comparing a TDS entry in 26AS against a credit in the bank statement, checking whether the AIS interest figure matches what the bank actually paid, and flagging anything that doesn't add up — because if you miss a mismatch, the notice lands on your client's doorstep.

And the formats. Every bank has its own PDF layout. Some put the date in DD/MM/YYYY, others use "15 Jan 2024." Some have the balance in the last column, others in the third. Multi-line remarks that span two rows. Opening balances that appear in different places — or don't appear at all.

The real cost

It's not just your time. It's the mental load of holding all these cross-references in your head, across dozens of clients, hoping you don't miss the one mismatch that triggers a notice six months later.

What If You Could Build a Tool That Handles All of This?

Not someone else's tool, with someone else's assumptions about how you work. Your tool. One that understands Indian bank statement formats, knows what AIS reconciliation actually means, speaks the language of 26AS and Section 80C — because you built it with your domain expertise.

That's exactly what we're going to do. And you don't need to know how to code.
